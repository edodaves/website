About

Hi, I’m Edo Daves, an entrepreneur working on the part of a business most founders never touch: the founder’s mind.

My roots are in Trentino, in the Italian Alps, where I’m finishing a degree in civil engineering at the University of Trento. Alongside my studies, two brands took shape. Zenith Council is a private mastermind for entrepreneurs making five figures a month and up, with retreats in Marbella, Sardinia, Mykonos, Cyprus and Dubai, where 55 founders came together. Neural Recoding is my own methodology for high-performing entrepreneurs.

That methodology is now my main focus. It’s grounded in neuroscience and cognitive behavioral therapy, and built on one principle: most limits come from coded beliefs about who you are, not from a lack of skill or strategy. The brain rewrites those beliefs when reality contradicts what it predicted, and willpower alone doesn’t do that. The work is 1:1, with founders whose growth has hit an identity-level ceiling. Everything now comes together under Nous, one ecosystem of coaching, community, software and masterminds, designed to make it structurally difficult for a young founder to fail.

The world’s problems will be solved by people who are in their 20s today. Nous is where they become capable of it. It’s being built in public, with one goal: unlocking 1,000 founders who change the world.

To work together, join Zenith Council, or book me as a speaker, get in touch.

*10% of Zenith’s revenue goes into the Zenith Fund, a yearly fund that Zenith members vote on and put into one high-impact project.
